This sets your baseline before the programme starts. It covers your history and your day first, then your body, recovery, hormones, food, training and protocol as they stand today. It ends with a personality inventory and three short scales. It takes around 45 minutes. Answers save as you type, so you can stop and come back.

How to answer

Give exact current figures, including the unflattering ones. If you can't remember precisely, give your best estimate. Leave nothing blank. Score the ratings for how things are now. You will score the same items again at the end of each block.

What I already hold

Your enquiry form from October 2025, the bloods you sent, and the summary I wrote from them. Sections 09 and 10 are filled in from that form. Correct each row, add the dates, delete anything you no longer take, and add anything missing.

Sections 18 to 21

These are published self-report scales, reproduced with their original wording. I use them as a starting measure to compare against later. They are not a diagnosis.

How to send it back

When you're done, tap Generate message, then Copy to clipboard, and paste it to me as a normal message. Don't send me the link: your answers are saved in your browser, so a link opens a blank form for me.

Disclaimer

This questionnaire is confidential and held for coaching use only. It is not medical advice. Pharmacological decisions require an independent prescribing physician familiar with your full history and do not substitute for clinical care.
